Featured

Psephos — a forecaster that is only allowed to call it when the evidence agrees

Elections, sport and world events. Independent methods each answer the question; a hard CALL needs two or more to agree past 95%. Otherwise it says WATCH and abstains, in public.

26/26calls right on the 3 Nov 2026 US midterms, from 71 races pre-registered
6/9calls right in a blind test on four 2026 Indian elections, forecast afterwards from pre-election data only
28/28calls right in a back-test of 46 Indian state elections, 2013–2025
0.126Brier score over 202 scored probabilities (0.25 = coin flip)

How the record is kept honest

Every forecast goes into an append-only ledger with a SHA-256 hash before the outcome, and the hashes are stamped to Bitcoin via OpenTimestamps. Every probability is scored, abstentions included, because a hit rate on calls alone can be gamed.

What the instruments are

A structural vote model built from decades of results, a poll model corrected by each pollster’s measured error, de-vigged prediction-market prices, and an LLM research forecaster that is barred from seeing market odds.

Where it lost, published on the site. All 3 misses in the blind test came from one brand-new party in Tamil Nadu; a new-party rule went into the engine within a week. On US races, prediction markets beat my structural model (Brier 0.033 vs 0.093), and the app says so. On the night of the midterms, every upset sat in a race the engine had refused to call.

How I work

  1. Define success before building. I agree the goal and how it will be measured up front, and lock forecasts and test cases before the result is known — so nothing is judged after the fact.
  2. Build AI that admits what it doesn’t know. My systems refuse rather than guess: the forecaster abstains when the evidence disagrees, WealthPilot labels AI output as an estimate, and my document assistant won’t answer beyond its verified sources.
  3. Show the misses, not just the wins. The failures, and the benchmarks I lose, sit on the same page as the successes.
  4. Own it end to end. Research, data, models, interface and deployment — one person, with API keys and costs kept server-side.
